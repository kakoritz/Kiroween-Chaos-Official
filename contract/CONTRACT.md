# Contract v1: IDs, catalogs, versioning

`contractVersion: 1`. **The protocol itself (events, endpoints, ordering, shapes, mock behavior) is in [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md).** Beat-by-beat fixtures are in [STORYBOARDS.md](STORYBOARDS.md). This file only holds the shared IDs and catalogs, so nothing is defined twice.

Server: `http://127.0.0.1:7070`. In the browser target the same operations are in `window.__haunt`.

## IDs

- `lane`: integer `1` to `5`
- `stage`: orders app `intake | account | payment | confirmation | dispatch | updates | tracking | handoff`; inbox app `fetch | classify | ticket | draft | reply | archive`
- `order`: `ord-0001` style (inbox: `msg-0001`)
- `wave`: integer from 1
- `tier`: `rookie | retrier | journaler | reconciler | phoenix`
- `part`: `retry | journal | idempotency | breaker | offline_queue`
- effect `kind`: `account_create | charge | email | dispatch | notify | ticket`

## Fault ids (technical)

`crash`, `out_of_memory`, `power_outage`, `internet_outage`, `endpoint_down`, `timeout`, `comm_timeout`, `slow_link`, `http_404`, `http_429`, `http_500`, `missing_file`, `duplicate_request`, `malformed_payload`, `disk_full`, `hazard_event`, `driver_unreachable`. Boss faults: `boss_power_outage`, `boss_internet_outage`, `boss_hazard_storm`.

## Versioning

- `contractVersion` is in `GET /api/health` and the first SSE message (`hello`).
- Additive changes (new fields, new event types, new endpoints) need no version bump. Clients ignore unknown fields and unknown event types.
- A rename, removal or change of meaning bumps the version and needs both lanes to agree.
- Any change to files in `contract/` goes in its own PR labelled `contract`, approved by the other lane.
