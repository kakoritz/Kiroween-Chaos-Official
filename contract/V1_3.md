# Contract v1.3 (additive, `contractMinor: 3`)

Adds to `PROTOCOL.md`, `CONTRACT.md`, `V1_1.md` and `V1_2.md`. `contractVersion` stays 1. Source: `.kiro/specs/modular-engine` (M3). Owner approved.

## 1. Apps declare their own stages
- `stageId` is no longer a closed list. A stage id is any name matching `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{1,31}$`. Every id that was valid before is still valid.
- `orderId` takes any 2 to 6 letter prefix: `^[a-z]{2,6}-[0-9]{4}$`. `ord-` and `msg-` still work.
- `hello.app` and `wave_start.detail.app` are app ids matching `^[a-z][a-z0-9_-]{1,31}$` (the three built-in ids still work).
- `hello.stages` (1 to 24 items) is the app's stage list: `{id, label, role?, kind?, icon?, workAnimation?, effects?: [{kind, key?}]}`. `role` is `input | work | effect | poll | final`. `kind` is the older four-way kind (V1_2 section 1).
- Rule 19 now reads: a `stage` in an event is one of the stage ids the app declared (`hello.stages`) or, when `hello` has none, the built-in app's list.
- The validator checks a stream of an app it does not know against that app's `hello.stages`.

- `chaos.detail.creatureName` (optional string): the app's own name for the creature, for example `Buffer Wraith`. `creature` stays the engine's creature id (sprite and rules). Set when the app's manifest has a `creatures:` entry for that fault at that stage.

## 2. What did not change
- Event types, envelope, ordering rules 1 to 52, endpoints and the fake provider wire format.
- Stage ids and order ids of Orders, Ticket Desk and Stream.

## 3. Why
A new service plugs in without a contract edit. The harness attaches faults by the stage's role (not its name), so any app gets chaos, scoring and a fix spec.
