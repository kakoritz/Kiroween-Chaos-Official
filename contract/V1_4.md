# Contract v1.4 (additive, `contractMinor: 4`)

Adds to `PROTOCOL.md`, `CONTRACT.md`, `V1_1.md`, `V1_2.md` and `V1_3.md`. `contractVersion` stays 1. Source: `docs/proposals/open-effect-kinds.md`. Owner approved.

## 1. Effect kinds are open
- `effect.detail.kind` and `repeat.detail.kind` are no longer a closed list. A kind is any name matching `^[a-z][a-z0-9_]{1,31}$`.
- Every kind that was valid before still is: `account_create`, `order`, `charge`, `email`, `store`, `dispatch`, `notify`, `ticket`, `refund`, `discount`.
- The fake provider takes a new route `POST /effects/<kind>` for any such kind. `tracking` and `handoff` are reserved and refused. The ten old routes are unchanged.
- A ledger row keeps the kind it was written with. A repeat is still (order, kind, index).

## 2. What did not change
- Event types, envelope, ordering rules, endpoints, ids and the provider's old routes and wire format.

## 3. Why
A service with its own kind of effect (`sms`, `ledger_entry`) shows it by name in the arena and the ledger, instead of being counted as `store`.
