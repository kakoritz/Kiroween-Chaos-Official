# Storyboards: the exact beats fixtures and demo scripts must follow

Times are **approximate offsets in seconds**, generated by `tools/make-fixtures.ts` from the engine duration model (`engine/design.md` section 1) with `pace: show` (stage durations x2), so a fixture and a live demo match. Beat order and event types are normative, exact seconds are not. Wave: seed 7, app `orders`, 5 lanes, race tiers, 4 orders. Lanes: 1 Rookie, 2 Retrier, 3 Journaler, 4 Reconciler, 5 Phoenix.

## calm.jsonl (about 60 s)

All five lanes process orders 1 to 4 cleanly. No chaos. Scores differ only by the speed bonus. Ends with `wave_end`. Purpose: the UI's idle and happy path.

## stacked-chaos.jsonl (about 50 s)

Lane 5 only, Payment stage. Presses at t=10 (Zombie `timeout`), t=11.5 (Ghost `http_404` during recovery, `scope:inner`, depth 2), t=13 (Reaper `crash`, depth 3). Expected: depth badge 1, 2, 3. After the crash: `process_dead`, `process_up`, replay, reconcile. The order completes clean. `score` includes the survival bonus 25 + 50 + 75 = 150. Other lanes calm.

## boss-power.jsonl (about 40 s)

`boss_warning{boss:boss_power_outage, startsInMs:8000}` at t=10, `boss_start` at t=18, one `chaos{creature:lich, source:boss}` and `process_dead{restartInMs:5000}` per lane, `process_up` at t=23, recovery per tier (table A7 in acceptance), `boss_end` at t=35. Lanes 4 and 5 clean. Lanes 1, 2 REPEAT. Lane 3 REPEAT if the crash point was before the journal write.

## meltdown.jsonl (about 60 s)

Lane 1 takes `malformed_payload` (Witch) at intake: `chaos`, `stage_fail`, `lane_halted`, then `order_abandoned` for each remaining order. Lane 2 takes `http_429` at payment: three `stage_fail`, then `order_failed`. Lane 5 takes the same two faults and ends with `order_quarantined` and a clean `order_done` on the next order. Purpose: failure states in the UI (X, red flash, sad pose, halted lane, rank board change).

## Fixture rules

- Event order obeys the ordering rules in `PROTOCOL.md`.
- Every fixture starts with `hello` and `wave_start` and ends with `wave_end`.
- Timestamps are real offsets so a replay at 1x feels right. The mock replays at `speed` x.
- Demo and fixture `chaos` events carry `source: "demo"`.

## full.jsonl (about 165 s, 11 orders)

1. 0 to 70 s: the hero-crash beats.
2. 70 to 105 s: the stacked-chaos beats on lane 5.
3. 105 to 150 s: the boss-power beats (Lich), all five lanes.
4. 150 to 170 s: remaining orders finish, `wave_end`. The UI then offers the postmortem.

## loop.jsonl (about 130 s)

2 lanes (lane 1 Rookie, lane 2 Phoenix), 4 orders per wave, a crash at Payment (`after_effect_before_journal`) on every order of both lanes.
1. Wave 1: crash at Payment on both. Rookie repeats the charge and the account, Phoenix reconciles. `wave_end`.
2. `hardening_spec{spec:"hardening-run1", title, path, findings}`.
3. `fix_applied{spec, files:[{path:"apps/haunted-orders/src/bots/rookie.ts", add, del}], recorded:true}`.
4. `part_added` for `retry`, `journal`, `idempotency` on lane 1 (they carry wave 1, rule 26).
5. Wave 2 with the same crash: lane 1 now reconciles and is clean. `wave_end`.
6. The UI shows the compare of wave 1 and wave 2 (grade F to A).
