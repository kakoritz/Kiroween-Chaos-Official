## Found by the first live run (2026-11-25)
- The overnight-style e2e `modular` section ran once by hand: 42 of 43 passed. The one failure was real: two `store` effects in one Playback order (license and finish) read as a repeat, because the provider's repeat identity is (order, kind, index) and the generic bot gave both index 1. Fixed: the generic bot numbers each kind per order, and the provider records an index for any kind. Test: `apps/generic/test/playback.test.ts`.
- The splash button "Test your own workflow" opened the hidden sprite lab; it now opens the Workflow Lab, and a new button opens the Chain Wizard.

## Found by the tournament probe (2026-11-26)
`node tools/probe-app.mjs <app>` plays a whole tournament on a registered app with the real engine. First runs found five Orders leaks, all fixed: the storm used Orders stages (`rounds.ts`), curses fell back to `payment` (`curses.ts`), a deferred fault walked the Orders order (`chaos/catalog.ts`), the engine treated every registered app as Orders (`runner/engine.ts`), the Casebook and corpses fell back to `payment` (`fallback-stage.ts`), and the generic bot's effect keys did not match the provider's repeat identity. Coffee Shop and Playback now pass 11 of 11. Tests: `packages/haunt/test/tournament-registered-app.test.ts`.
